Plateia Ecarcheion
37.98665411893884, 23.734917460917234
A sculpture in the center, a few trees in the square, people

People have gathered in a circle around the sculpture. They are busy chatting. Then, without warning, everything falls silent. There is only a premonition that something is about to begin. The people gradually lower their voices, turning their eyes toward the sculpture in the center. From high up in the trees planted around the sculpture, a combination of notes (Bb-F-Bb-D-F) can be heard. When the sound rings out, the people begin talking again, as if they have understood that this was not what was supposed to begin.

A man appears

The man moves in small steps around the sculpture, tracing a circle with it at the center. He raises his right hand, gripping the air as if holding a strap on a bus, and seems to be saying something from time to time, but nothing reaches the people. Bumping into the sculpture now and then, he keeps going round and round for a while. Then the man disappears into the crowd.
The people found no charm in his actions, yet could not look away.

The Sarcophagus of Alexander
37.99351228369035, 23.735796930960028

A sculpture in the center of the square, a dozen or so cats around it, three people smoking on a bench in the back

From the right side, a mass of cigarette smell is drifting this way. The smell breaks off at times and reappears at others. The shape of the smell suspended in the air is something like a bubble.

The moment the shape of the smell is drawn clearly in my mind, all the cats begin to cry. As if trying to match each other's voices, their scattered cries gradually converge into a single note of low precision.

The three people on the bench put out their cigarettes.

The cats gradually stop crying.

From far away, the sound of a motorcycle engine echoes.

The cats stop crying completely.


Philopappos Hill
37.967263132340506, 23.72163957791652

On top of the hill, a man, a kite, a restaurant below the hill

Three months ago, the man was blessed with a baby boy. Six months ago, the man had begun building a large kite in his workshop. From the very first day he started, the plan was already perfect in his head — what materials to use, how to construct it, what picture to paint on it. No one had told him to do it. For some reason, he simply believed it would be for the good of the child about to be born, and so he began. Neither his wife nor his colleagues found it particularly strange, and no one tried to stop him by calling it pointless. And so the kite was completed. It measured three by three meters, and painted on it were the child's name and a sky full of stars. The man told his wife he had made a reservation at the restaurant below the hill.

At the reserved time, his wife and child were at the restaurant.

The man, together with ten companions, flew the kite on top of the hill.

The kite soared powerfully into the sky. But the man had failed.

From where his wife sat, she could only see the back of the kite.

The sky full of stars and the child's name were visible only to the man.


Inside a restaurant
37.96961217825035, 23.723291122546353
Inside a restaurant, a round table with a tablecloth, three people, each holding a different drink

The signal was the thud of grilled sardines being placed at the center of the table. The person holding tsipouro began to sing in a loud voice. The person holding beer waited a moment, gauged the timing, then began to sing even louder. The person holding tea began pounding the table.

Next, grilled squid arrived, and the table went thud again.
In that instant, the person holding tsipouro fell silent. A great singing voice was ringing inside that person's head, so they did not notice they had gone quiet.
Then the person holding beer fell silent. A great sound was ringing inside that person's head. Following suit, the person holding tea stopped pounding the table.

Inside each of their heads, they could also hear the sounds ringing inside the others' heads, so all three remained silent and played their music.



A workshop
37.96176838798409, 23.731607461644405
A woman, a kite on the workshop table, the child is at the stepmother's

The woman had a husband. A normally hardworking, gentle man who one day came to her with something that sounded like madness. He was going to build a large kite for their unborn child. At first she took it as just another new hobby, but day by day his seriousness only grew. From where I stood, it seemed he was consumed by the idea of "doing something" for the child. There was no coherence between the means and the purpose, but "the fact of doing something" was what he needed.

The woman looks at the kite on the table. Then she quietly takes a pencil and writes, faintly on the back, the names of her husband, herself, and their child.
